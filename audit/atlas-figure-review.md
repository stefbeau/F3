# Atlas pages: review of the reported figures against their originals

Status of each reported figure on the atlas pages (D-067). Updated by hand or by the scheduled review; every pass adds a dated line to the log at the end and never deletes an earlier one.

**Codes.** `O` read in the original study, report or official record · `P` read in the publisher's or the authors' own release or abstract, not the full text · `S` secondary only: a news report or summary, original not opened · `W` weak: the source gives no origin for the figure, or the source is informal · `X` differed from the original and was corrected on the page (see the note).

**Scope of pass 1 (2026-11-25).** 34 pages opened or searched. Headline figures of ten topics. Not checked in this pass: creative work and copyright (all `S`), the figures marked `S` below, and every figure not listed.
