# D-060 — Full-scope data campaign: AI impact on all human and nature systems

**Date:** 2026-10-26
**Editor:** Stéphane Beau
**Status:** Open

## Motivation
F3's scope is the impact of AI on all aspects of human and nature life.
The D-058 campaign covered the model's existing links (L3–L7, DC, M5, AI).
D-060 extends to the full breadth of AI's planetary footprint, including
domains not yet modelled: extreme weather, biodiversity, food systems,
human health (full), migration, education, geopolitics, financial stability,
social cohesion, and AI safety.

## Findings from registration (added by Claude, 2026-10-27, corrected the same day; facts from public pages, no downloads)
Several licences and details in the brief did not match the public pages. Each register entry holds the detail; the ones that change a decision above:
- **Licences differ from the brief:** SPEIbase is ODbL 1.0 (share-alike), not CC BY 4.0; FAOSTAT is CC BY 4.0 under FAO's database terms, not CC BY-NC 3.0 IGO; BACI is Etalab 2.0, not CC BY; V-Dem is CC BY-SA 4.0, not CC BY 4.0; OECD.AI shows "all rights reserved". GFW states that each dataset carries its own licence.
- **Unconfirmed (HTTP 403, redirect or JavaScript-only pages):** IHME GBD, UNHCR, OECD TiVA, ENTSO-E, IDMC and ACLED terms were not read.
- **GRACE-FO:** the current JPL product on the page is RL06.3 v04, not RL06.1; the product-selection question above should be re-asked with that in mind.
- **BACI** is a CEPII database of all products at HS 6-digit level, not an FAO agri-food database.
- **`nsidc_permafrost`:** the first address (G02171) is a sea-ice chart product and the corrected address (NSIDC-0422) is an Antarctic elevation model; the permafrost map is GGD318, now registered. **`cset_export_control`:** both URLs given returned HTTP 404; the CSET report Tracking AI Chips is registered as the best match, pending the editor's confirmation.
