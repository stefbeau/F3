# D-062 — "Average or spread?": a hypothesis page with three infographics in the Explorer

**Date:** 2026-11-12
**Editor:** Stéphane Beau
**Status:** Proposed (not approved; branch `site-spread`, not merged, not published)

## What is proposed
A ninth page in the Explorer, `spread.html` (navigation label "Spread"), built by `site_src/spread_body.py`. It carries three inline SVG infographics in the
style of `site_src/ig.py` and states one hypothesis: AI narrows the gap between strong and weak performers on short, supervised tasks and widens it on long
tasks delegated to agents. The editor asked on 2026-11-12 for the visuals of his article on this subject to be adapted for the Explorer.

## What the page is not
- **Not an F3 result.** Nothing on the page is computed by the model and nothing is built into it. A notice at the top says so.
- **Not a tested claim.** The hypothesis is labelled untested, and the page says that most published evidence so far shows gaps narrowing.
- **Not a model change.** The last section ("proposed, not built") lists three ideas for F3; none is decided here.

## Verification level (reported as it is)
- AI studies (Brynjolfsson, Li and Raymond; Dell'Acqua et al.; Otis et al.): figures read from search extracts of the papers' own pages on 2026-11-12.
- Six other systems (rainfall, firm productivity, incomes, political polarization, heat extremes, armed conflict): figures read from search extracts on
  2026-11-12. **The primary pages were not opened**, so none of these rows is "verified" in the sense of `research/SOURCES.md`. Three links are summaries
  (AAAS press summary, the authors' VoxEU column, the Hong Kong Observatory's summary of IPCC AR6), not the primary publication.
- The reading of heat extremes as "mostly a shifted average" is an inference made for this page, not a quotation. The page says so.
- The repository already holds UCDP conflict counts (`audit/data/derived/ucdp_conflicts_per_year.json`); the page's figure of 65 for 2025 was **not**
  compared with that file.

## Alternatives considered
1. Put the infographics on the Story page: rejected, the Story page shows model output and this is not model output.
2. Publish only in the article outside the repository: possible; costs nothing to choose later (delete the page and the navigation entry).
3. A page (this proposal): keeps the hypothesis next to its caveats and sources, separate from model output.

## Cost of reversing
Small: remove `site_src/spread_body.py`, `tests/test_site_spread.py`, the `spread.html` entries in `site_src/pages.py`, six lines of `site_src/style.css`
and `site/spread.html`, then restamp. No data and no model code depend on it.
