## 7. What "other" is for (D-016: not assessed)

Finding (from `audit/data/earth4all_cross_sector_links.csv` and the port): the only variable of "other" that any sector reads is `CTA` (cost of the turnarounds = cost of food + cost of energy), read by public: `ECTAF2022 = max(0, CTA - CTA2022)`, which sets the TFP loss from unprofitable activity (`PLUA`, `RTFPUA`) and, through demand's extra taxes (`ETTAF2022`), the government's budget. The other six variables (`FB15`, `PB15`, `LK`, `IEL`, `RGGDPP`, `PGDPP`) are performance indicators with no downstream use. So "other" matters for the economy through one variable and is ported (7 equations, 1 state); it is within 0.011% in every run. Its input `COFO` (cost of food) comes from the replaced foodland sector: D-023 notes World3 agriculture has no equivalent, which is an open point for M6.
