## 3. Why — what was measured and what was not

- **Measured, diagnostic (labelled; it changes no S3 equation):** when S3's composite task input `T` is replaced, period by period, by the stored `cognitive_ces_term`, **every compared quantity passes at every year in all three settings**: output 3e-16, capital 7e-16, automation 2e-15, hardware up to 1.8e-4, software up to 1.5e-3, compute stock up to 1.4e-4. So S3's laws of motion for compute, hardware, software and capital, its automation function, its timing and its output formula match the shipped runs; **the whole failure comes from the production composite `T` (eqs. 14 to 16, 26 to 28) and from what that feeds back** (output → investment dollars → stocks).
- **Measured:** `T` matches in the first two periods (about 0.1% to 0.3%, while automation is still at its initial value), and falls below the stored value from 2027, when `f` leaves `f_init`: output is 5.7% low in 2027 and the gap compounds through the investment feedback.
- **Not established (unresolved, reported):** *why* my composite differs. Candidates: the solver's task-grid discretisation (paper footnote 38 gives 20 and 100 grid points; the stored per-task allocation has 101 entries, which does not match), the stored allocation being a different iterate from the one that produced `T`, or my reading of eqs. 14 to 16 and footnote 39. Section 9.1 identity 8 of `research/gate-equations.md` recorded this before the run: a reconstruction from the stored allocation also drifts by up to ~15% by 2036. I have **not** tried the solver's grids or any other variant, because that would be an equation change after seeing the data. It is the first thing to try if the editor decides to.
- **Equation 10 / the amendment-B classification.** Stored `compute` is the raw stock `Q` (identity 1, committed before the run), and the stored `compute / software` exceeding `C_L` is therefore not evidence against eq. 10. I have no evidence that the playground differs from the paper on eq. 10, so I did not apply "playground differs from the paper" to anything. The first year `Q > 0.1 C_L` in S3 is 2062 (default), 2094 (conservative), 2063 (aggressive); the stored `compute/software > C_L/11` cross-check gives 2049, 2058, 2045 and is **not** the same condition (it is `Q/S`).
- **In-sample caveat.** The timing and discretisation choices were identified from the same series S3 is compared with, so the matches of hardware, software, capital, stock and automation are close to in-sample by construction; the only independent test is `T` and the 76-period chain, and that is what failed.
