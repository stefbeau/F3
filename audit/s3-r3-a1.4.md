## 1. Headline (the numbers are poor, and R3 was not tuned)

- **Shares.** R3 reproduces the average shape but not the timing. Largest share errors in percentage points: capital 22 to 24 pp, training share 42 to 55 pp (from 2025: the curve starts at 75% while the shipped runs start at 66% to 90%, and the training share falls off a cliff in GATE once automation is complete, which a function of `f` and `u` smooths), compute 6 to 16 pp, hardware R&D up to 21 pp (default, late years), software R&D 2.7 to 26 pp (conservative, 2093 to 2096). Mean errors are 0.1 to 10 pp.
- **Composite-independent quantities, with Epoch's stored composite `T` supplied (set b), i.e. the rule's own error:** automation fraction is within 9.5% at worst (2.95% aggressive, 8.2% default, 9.5% conservative; mean under 1%). Hardware 30% and 35% off at worst in default and aggressive (6% in conservative), software 5.7% to 14% at worst, **effective compute (derived) 43% to 72% off at worst with mean errors of 4% to 12%**, output 13% to 20% at worst. So even with the open composite removed, **R3 as fitted does not reproduce effective compute or output to a few percent**.
- **Full chain (set a):** output, raw stock `Q`, hardware and software are off by 70% to 100% at worst and by more than 5% from 2026 to 2029, which is the A1.3 composite failure plus the rule. Automation stays within 11% (3% aggressive).
- **No criterion applies**, so none is failed; the figures say R3's default calibration is a rough representation of GATE's paths, not a reproduction.

## 2. What comes from the rule and what from the open composite

Compare set (a) with set (b) for the same setting:
- The **output gap collapses from 100% to 13% to 20% at worst** once the stored composite is supplied, and the capital-share error halves (22 to 11 pp, default): most of the full-chain error is the open composite (A1.3, D-027 outcome ii), not the rule.
- What remains in (b) is the rule's own error: the share errors (identical in kind in (a) and (b): they are differences between R3's curves and the shipped shares, by percentage points), and the effective-compute and hardware errors they cause. The largest rule-driven errors are the training share at the start (a starting value of about 75% against 66% to 90%) and the **timing of the capital switch-on**, which in GATE depends on its own path and in R3 on `f`.
- The shipped shares themselves are not converged optima (solver tolerance 0.02), so some of the "rule error" is the reference's noise; this report cannot separate that.

## 3. Method notes and caveats

- **In-sample:** the curves are fitted to these same three runs; differences are what three curves of `f` and `u` cannot represent, not out-of-sample error. Calibrating to the three playground paths calibrates to Epoch's playground, not to a truth about investment behaviour (D-028).
- Effective compute is not stored; the reference row is **derived** as `g(stored compute) x stored software` with eq. 10 (labelled so in the tables). The stored `compute` is the raw stock `Q` (`research/gate-equations.md` 9.1).
- Set (b) feeds the stored composite `T` into S3 (a labelled diagnostic input, `stored_T`, not a model option).
- Shares are compared in percentage points (several shipped shares are near zero, so relative differences would be meaningless); the consumption share is the remainder.
- `R3` was **not** tuned after seeing these numbers. If the editor wants a better fit, that is a new, labelled post-hoc proposal (for example: knots in `f` closer together near 0.1 to 0.5, or fitting the three runs separately for a parameter-dependent curve family).
