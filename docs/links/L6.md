# L6 — Energy-system emissions to climate (draft, D-058)

**Status of this page:** data and literature plan, written before any code or test run. No result is reported. Provisional (delegated). Link numbering as in L3.md. Under D-021 option A emissions are currently prescribed (the FaIR "medium-extension" scenario); L6 is the unbuilt link that would replace that with emissions computed by F3's energy and economy sectors.

## What must connect
Fossil and industrial CO2 and non-CO2 emissions from S4/S2 (and land use) to the FaIR emissions inputs of `s6_climate`, with the same species, units and time convention (D-022: t = Y is 1 January of year Y; FaIR inputs are mid-year, 1750.5 to 2500.5).

## Candidate variables and units
| Variable | Unit | Source series |
|---|---|---|
| Fossil CO2 | Mt CO2/yr (FaIR uses GtC or GtCO2 per file: check species config) | GCB 2025 extract (2015-2024, in the repo), `ceds_global_emissions` (1750-2023), EDGAR (fossil: NC-ND, raw only) |
| CH4, N2O, SO2, BC, OC, NH3, NOx, CO, VOC | Mt per species | `ceds_global_emissions`, `edgar_global_non_co2_by_sector` |
| CO2 concentration | ppm | `noaa_co2_annual` (1959-2025) |
| Observed warming | K | `hadcrut5_global_annual` |
| Electricity CO2 intensity | gCO2/kWh | `ember_electricity_world` (458 in 2025) |
| Data-centre electricity | TWh | see L5.md |
| Emissions pathways | per species | FaIR extension file `extensions_1750-2500.csv` (pinned; original source and licence not stated) |

## Functional forms in the literature
1. **Emissions = activity x intensity** per fuel and sector (Kaya-type identity; CEDS builds emissions from fuel use and emission factors, Hoesly et al. 2018) [from memory, unverified against the retrieved paper].
2. **FaIR impulse-response with carbon-cycle feedbacks** (Leach et al. 2021; calibrated ensemble from the pinned 841-member file): the form is fixed by D-021; only the inputs are new.
3. **Non-CO2 emissions as ratios to CO2 or to sector activity**, since F3 has no methane or aerosol sector. The EDGAR sector split gives the candidate ratios.
4. **Scenario scaffolding** (RCMIP, Nicholls et al. 2020): harmonise to historical emissions at the join year (2023 or 2024) [from memory, unverified].

## Calibration data available (retrieved)
CEDS v2025_03_18 (1750-2023, CC BY 4.0); EDGAR 2025 non-CO2 by sector (CC BY 4.0); GCB 2025 summary in the repo and cross-checks: GCB versus OWID 0.002%, versus CEDS 1.6%, versus EDGAR 2.7% for world fossil CO2; EDGAR versus CEDS methane 2.5% (cross-checks recorded). FaIR input files re-downloaded from the pinned commit are byte-identical to the audit's. Concentrations and warming for the check.

## What is missing
- **Full Global Carbon Budget workbook** (land-use CO2, sinks): ICOS click-through, not accepted by the campaign.
- **IIASA SSP database** (login): the origin of the FaIR extensions file is not documented.
- National inventories (UNFCCC portal is a JavaScript shell), Climate TRACE (other sectors not retrieved), Energy Institute.
- Land-use CO2 history in a form F3 can use.
- A rule for non-CO2 species when energy mix changes (no data to calibrate it).

## Pass/fail test (written without results)
1. **Join continuity.** With computed emissions replacing prescribed ones, the 2023-2025 species emissions match the prescribed series within a tolerance fixed in advance (propose 2% for CO2, 10% for CH4; the editor sets them). Fail otherwise.
2. **Warming consequence.** The 2015-2024 ensemble-mean warming is unchanged within the existing A2.1 offset (0.037 K below HadCRUT5; D-030): the link may not move the historical period.
3. **Mass conservation.** Cumulative CO2 emitted in 2025-2100 from the energy sector equals the FaIR input cumulative.
4. **Switch-off invariance:** with computed emissions replaced by the prescribed series the sector returns the stand-alone FaIR result to the recorded tolerance.
5. Labels D-030 stay: climate starts 0.04-0.07 K below observed and non-CO2 is prescribed unless a decision changes it.
