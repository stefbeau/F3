# M6 minimal integration: readings and tests, fixed BEFORE the wiring exists (D-051; `docs/m6-minimal-plan.md`)

**Status:** written 2026-10-21 on branch `m6-minimal`, before `f3/sectors/m6_integrated.py` exists. Tests: `tests/test_m6_integration.py`. The damage coupling itself (tests in `tests/test_m6_damage_coupling.py`, 43 tests) was written and passed first. Nothing here compares S3's absolute output with GATE or with data (plan section 15.2: structural tests only, shapes not levels).

## What is wired (one loop, step 0.25 year, t0 = 1900, t1 = 2100)

| Sector (name) | Starts | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `world3` (the D-031 group; S1, agriculture, capital, resources, pollution) | 1900 | **input `warming` only when the damage coupling is ON** (D-051); otherwise no inputs |
| `s6_climate` | 2025 | the **recorded replay** (exact: emissions prescribed, D-021 A), or a live `S6Climate` for the run-time measurement |
| `s3_ai` under R3, `s4_datacentre`, `earth4all_supply` (recorded series) | 2025 | the M2 slice wiring unchanged; held at their initial values before 2025 (D-020: "before 2025 the automation fraction is held at GATE's initial value") by a start-delay wrapper |
| `s7_feed` (adapter), `s7_given` (constants), `s7_social` | 2025 | S7 **output-only**: nothing reads it |

**The start-delay wrapper** (`DelayedStart`): before its start year the wrapped sector publishes its initial outputs unchanged and does not advance; from the start year it is the wrapped sector, byte for byte. **Adapters (labelled):** `s7_feed` turns S3's `labor_share` (its initial value before the first annual period is the no-automation value `1 - alpha` = 0.65, since S3 publishes none yet) and S3's `gwp / labor` into S7's inputs `labor_share` and `gdp_per_capita_growth` (the growth rate of `gwp / labor` between annual values; one-year lag from the explicit coupling); `s7_given` supplies `unemployment` = 0 and `redistribution` = 0 as **illustrative given inputs**. **No economy reconciliation:** S3's economy and World3's are two models of the same world in one run (plan 14.2 item 2); stated on every output.

## Tests, with the output and window NAMED HERE in advance

**A6.1 baseline.** Damage coupling OFF, S3, FaIR and S7 present but not coupled to World3: **every one of the 29 World3 states** in the integrated loop (a) equals the World3 group run alone **to 1e-9**, both parameter sets, every year 1900-2100, and (b) is within **2%** of WorldDynamics.jl at every 0.5-year reference point (D-004; the existing A3.2 criterion; the reference fixtures), both sets.

**A6.3 invariance (each coupling switched off returns the stand-alone result).** (1) FaIR to World3 damage: OFF equals the stand-alone group to 1e-9 (already in `test_m6_damage_coupling.py`; repeated inside the integrated loop). (2) FaIR to S3 damage (`s3_damage="off"`) with the data-centre coupling off (`electricity=False`) equals stand-alone S3 driven by R3 (`S3AI(rule=R3Rule())` run alone) to **1e-9** for `gwp`, `compute_stock`, `frac_automated`. (3) S3 to S7: S7 inside the loop driven by S3 equals S7 stand-alone driven by **the same input paths** (recorded from the integrated run through a replay) to **1e-9**. (4) S6 live (20 members) equals the recorded replay (recorded from the same live run) for S3's and World3's outputs to 1e-9, horizon to 2030.

**A6.4 extreme conditions (structure only).** (a) Zero compute growth: **correction of 2026-10-21, made before any M6 integration result existed:** the first version of this line said that with R3 `initial_ai_share = 0` the demand "stays at its 2025 value". That was wrong: with zero AI investment the compute stock decays by depreciation, so demand falls. The A2.5 form (a constant compute source into the data-centre module keeps its demand at 485 TWh) is already tested in `tests/test_m2_slice.py` and is repeated unchanged. The integrated-loop test is instead: with `initial_ai_share = 0` the compute stock obeys `Q[k] = Q[k-1] exp(-0.3)` to 1e-9 from 2026 on (the identity measured for GATE's stock, `research/gate-equations.md` 9.1, with zero compute investment), the data-centre demand is non-increasing, and nothing is NaN. (b) Unlimited electricity (`supply_scale = inf`): `supply_ratio` is 1 in every year (B1 inactive). (c) Zero investment (all R3 AI shares 0 and `capital_scale` 0): the compute stock falls by depreciation and is never negative, and S3's capital stock never goes negative. (d) Over the grid {World3 damage off/central/high} x {S3 damage off/central/high} x {warming path recorded} finiteness of **every output of every sector** and non-negativity of the stocks.

**A6.2 loop sign tests (outputs and windows named now).**
- **R1** (more AI investment, more automation): `s3_ai.frac_automated` at **the years 2029, 2030 and 2031**, race setting (`initial_ai_share` 1.5 x fitted, `decay_speed` 0.5) **above** low setting (0.5 x fitted), **strictly at all three years**. *Disclosure:* this output replaces the one that failed in the M2 slice (the year automation reaches 0.5, which is the same year in all settings); the window was chosen after seeing the M2 data (the settings differ before they saturate), so it is a pre-registered M6 test but not blind to the earlier result. **Control that can fail:** low against low must fail the strict assertion; a mutation (R3 levers ignored) must fail it.
- **B1** (less electricity, slower compute): `s3_ai.usable_compute` (the quantity B1 actually scales; the slice's test used the raw stock, and failed) at **each year 2027 to 2035**, half-supply run **at or below** the base run in every one of the nine years (supply binds from about 2027, before the stock feedback that appeared from 2038 in the slice). **The slice's B1 test failed as written; if this one fails it is recorded with size and first year and nothing is adjusted.** Control: base against base must fail the strict-below assertion (a strict inequality at least once in the window).
- **B2** (more damage, lower output): S3 `gwp` in **2100**, high damage option **below** low; **and** World3 industrial capital `ic` in **2100** with the World3 damage coupling ON-high **below** ON-low. Control: low against low fails.
- **R2 via S3** (automation, falling labour share, rising inequality index): the S7 `inequality` **change relative to the start in 2060** is **positive** with S3's labour share as the driver (central R3), and **zero** (to 1e-12) when S7 is driven by a constant labour share instead. *Structural:* S3's labour-share level is unvalidated (D-029); S7 uses only the relative fall.
- **B5** is S7's own test (`tests/test_s7_social.py`), not repeated here. **B3, B4 (S5) and B6 are not tested in M6-minimal** (plan section 3).

**A6.5 run time.** Measured and reported, no pass level: (i) the integrated run with the recorded climate replay, 1900-2100, three repeats; (ii) **one run with a live 841-member S6Climate (D-021 option a), once, in the background, output to a file**; the upper bound for a thousand runs is derived from (ii) and stated.

**A6.6 initialisation.** World3 from its own 1900 state (D-019 Option C), S6 from its history, S3 from GATE's initial values at 2025 (D-020), S7 from its documented illustrative start: a test checks each at the first step it exists.

## Rules
Failures are recorded with size and first year; nothing is adjusted after a result; the pre-registered sign tests have controls that must fail and mutations that must be caught.

## Dated note, 2026-10-21 (after the first run of the integration tests)
The R2-via-S3 control, as first coded, kept the real warming input while replacing the labour share by a constant. It failed: the inequality change in 2060 was 5.8e-4, not 0. Cause: a mistake in the test's construction, not in the model. Warming raises tension, tension above its threshold triggers the delayed policy response, and the response lowers inequality. The reading ("zero when S7 is driven by a constant labour share instead") meant to isolate the labour-share driver, so the control now holds warming at 0 as well, as S7's own R2 test does. The failing version is in commit a39237d. No threshold changed; the positive assertion (cause run, 2060) is unchanged and passed before the correction.
