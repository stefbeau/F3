# F3 Explorer v0 (branch `site`): what it is, and the choices made

**Status:** built 2026-10-21 on branch `site`; **not merged into main, not published, Pages not enabled, the workflow has not been run.** The editor reviews it in the browser: open `site/index.html` (the five pages link to each other and are self-contained; they open from disk).

## What it is
A static site (D-009: precomputed scenario grids, no backend). Five pages in `site/`: **index.html** (the Explorer), **methods.html**, **limitations.html**, **sources.html**, **about.html**, linked by a header and a footer navigation on every page, with the same CSS tokens, light and dark mode, English only. The Explorer has a title, one plain-English line, the scenario controls first, then three panels that are **not coupled to each other** (a banner says so): (1) World3 scenarios, (2) Climate (FaIR, 841 members), (3) AI race (automation and damage). Each panel has a one-line status visible without clicking; all caveat text is in a collapsed `<details>` under it. The "conditional scenarios, not forecasts" banner is on every page.

## Build
- Generator and templates are in `site_src/` (not in `site/`, so that what would be deployed is only pages and data): `generate.py`, `pages.py` (the five pages), `explorer.js` (charts and controls), `style.css`.
- Full build (about 2 minutes; needs the 841-member climate recording, `dashboard/demo0/record_climate.py`): `PYTHONPATH=. python site_src/generate.py <climate841.npz>`. Restamp only (date and commit hash): `... generate.py --reuse`.
- `site/data/explorer.json` holds everything the pages draw **and** the series that are withheld (S3 compute, data-centre electricity); `.github/workflows/pages.yml` deploys `site/` and is manual-only.

## What changed from Demo 0, and why (editor decision 2026-10-21, "fix all")
- **A. AI panel cut.** The S3 compute charts, the B1 compute chart and the data-centre electricity chart and table are **not rendered**: their values reach about 1.4e40 FLOP/year and 7.4e9 TWh/year. One visible note says they are withheld until the S3 compute path is validated (awaiting Epoch AI). Kept: the automation chart, the damage chart and table (shares of S3's unvalidated output; the "2100 output, USD/yr" column is dropped), and the loop-sign text without the large magnitudes. The series remain in `explorer.json` and the generator.
- **B. Charts.** Readable ticks ("2 billion", "0.5", "3.24 K"), unit label on its own line above the plot, text drawn at its real size (the SVG viewBox is the pixel size, measured minimum rendered font 13 px at 390 px width).
- **C. World3 and controls.** The grey dashed "standard" reference and its note appear only when a lever differs from standard. The AI controls say which chart each control changes. Each panel has its status line.
- **D. Pages.** Methods, Limitations, Sources, About (this page's statements are taken from the repository records).
- **E. Accessibility.** Colour-blind-safe palette (Okabe-Ito based), dash patterns on series with direct end labels, a `<title>`, `<desc>` and `aria-label` text summary on every chart, visible keyboard focus, selectors usable by keyboard.

## Checked (Edge, through its debugging protocol)
All five pages at 390 px and 1200 px, light and dark (20 measurements): horizontal scroll none (page scroll width equals client width; 1185 at 1200 px is the scrollbar), no element wider than the viewport, no script or console errors, every chart has a title and an aria-label, minimum rendered chart font 13 px. Interaction: a World3 lever changes the curves and shows the note and the dashed reference; the AI setting redraws the automation chart. Screenshots of the top of the page, the controls and each panel (light and dark, 390 px), the four other pages (light and dark, 390 px) and the AI panel at 1200 px were looked at. **Not checked:** a physical phone, browsers other than Edge, a screen reader (the summaries are in the markup but were not read aloud), colour-blindness simulation (the palette is a published colour-blind-safe set and every series also differs by dash pattern and end label).

## Caveat-text comparison with Demo 0
Sentence by sentence, the Demo 0 template's caveat text was compared with the five new pages (script `caveat_diff`, 31 specific caveat statements checked verbatim): **nothing was lost.** Intentional changes, all under A: (1) the withheld charts' own descriptions were kept but moved into the AI-race caveats in the past tense ("The IEA points were ..." instead of "Dots: ..."); (2) the table row "Model 2025 / Model 2030, demand / used" and the "2100 output, USD/yr" column are not rendered; (3) the heading "Demo 0" became "F3 Explorer v0" and the sentence "Every curve below ..." appears as "Every curve in the Explorer ..." on the subpages; (4) one wording was made more exact: the Epoch AI playground runs are no longer described as "(CC BY 4.0)" on the Explorer page, because Epoch's statement covers its work in general and no licence is stated for the playground's script bundle (Sources page).

## Provisional (delegated) decisions
- **D-046** (World3 levers and runs: 3 levers x 3 values, 27 runs, 2004 parameter set, D-031 group): see `DECISIONS.md` on this branch; only standard parameters are compared with WorldDynamics.jl.
- **D-047** (Explorer pages, withheld outputs and chart conventions, recorded on this branch): see `DECISIONS.md`.

## Known issues (not fixed)
- Dark-mode and light-mode charts are drawn when the page loads; if the colour scheme changes while the page is open the charts redraw, but a manual `data-theme` toggle does not exist (the CSS supports it, the page has no button).
- The Limitations table of failed tests is stacked blocks, not a table, on purpose (a table was unreadable at 390 px).
- The generated Explorer page and data are about 0.5 MB each and are committed on this branch.
- "Last updated" and the commit hash on the pages are those of the source commit at build time, so they name the commit before the one that contains the built pages; the About page says so.

Not merged; the Pages workflow is unchanged. The generator (`site_src/generate.py`) now builds 27 lever runs x 4 climate-damage states (108 World3 runs) with the merged M6 coupling and the recorded FaIR ensemble mean (no new FaIR run); `--reuse --built-commit <hash>` restamps the pages and names the commit that contains them on About.

- **B. - **C. Takeaway cards:** sentence and tiles computed by `w3Takeaway`, `climateTakeaway`, `aiTakeaway` from the data (`tests/test_site_ux.py` runs them in Node on the committed data and on synthetic data; hard-coded values would fail).
- **D. Presets, Reset, Custom, chips, URL hash:** each preset is a grid point; a bad hash falls back to defaults (damage off).
- **E. Charts:** hover, touch and keyboard crosshair with a readout; PNG and CSV download (with the caveats).
- **F. Infographics** (inline SVG, text alternatives): feedback loops (solid = built: warming to World3 output and warming to S3 output; dotted = built in the AI-race run, outputs withheld; dashed = not built), roadmap, warming stripes labelled with the A2.1 failure, status badges linking to Limitations. The tests check that exactly the built links are solid.
- **G. Layout:** sticky tab bar, "Read this first", definitions for S3, R3, FaIR, GATE and World3 (tap, click or keyboard, not only the title attribute).
- **Checked in Edge (390 and 1200 px, light and dark, 20 measurements):** no horizontal overflow, no console or script errors, no text below 13 px (two fixes made on the way: code and badge sizes). Interaction checks: damage on and off, presets and Reset, hash restore and bad hash, keyboard and touch readout, CSV and PNG download.
- **Known issues:** the status badges use three levels (amber, red; none green) chosen by me, listed in D-052; the CSV and PNG download were checked through the page's own download function, not through a browser save dialog.



## Explorer v0.2 (branch `site-ux`, D-056 Provisional, 2026-10-22): what changed
Not merged, not published: Pages is not enabled and the workflow has not been run. Editor feedback on v0.1: not user friendly, too few visualization tools. No new model number and no new model run: everything is drawn from `site/data/explorer.json` (the grid is unchanged: 108 World3 runs, 9 AI-race runs, the 841-member FaIR mean and band).

**Pages** (all static, all open from disk): `index.html` the story front door; `explorer.html` the dashboard; `compare.html`; `connections.html`; `methods.html`, `limitations.html`, `sources.html`, `about.html` (kept, restyled). Shared files: `assets/f3.css` (tokens, Work Sans embedded as base64), `assets/f3-core.js` (pure functions, loaded as `window.F3`, loadable in Node), `assets/f3-charts.js` (line and diverging-bar charts, data table, PNG, CSV, text alternative), one script per page, `assets/shell.js` (theme toggle, definitions), `data/explorer.js` (the same data as `explorer.json`, as a script so pages open from disk). `explorer.json` is still the source file; it contains `NaN` (valid JavaScript, not JSON), so Python reads it and Node tests load it as a literal.

**1. Shell and tokens.** Dark dashboard theme (Explorer, Compare, Connections) and light reading theme (story and text pages, which follow `prefers-color-scheme`); a toggle on every page, the choice kept in `localStorage` only (every access in try/catch). Palette: baseline #6B7280, scenario A #1F78B4, scenario B #D95F02, the same on every chart; hex codes are recorded in `docs/design-guidelines.md` 4.1. Work Sans (OFL 1.1, from the `@fontsource-variable/work-sans` package) is embedded, so there is no network request; serif system stack for headings on reading pages. `tests/test_site_tokens.py` computes WCAG contrast from the stylesheet (text 4.5:1 on three surfaces, lines 3:1, badges and buttons 4.5:1) and fails on any font size below 13 px.

**2. Story** (`index.html`): hero with a generated one-sentence message, a four-step sticky indicator, four chapters (the standard world turns down; add the heat; the AI race, automation timing only, levels flagged red; what is connected), each with a finding title generated from the data, one annotated chart and one control (starting resources; damage option; AI investment setting; which link status to highlight).

**3. Dashboard** (`explorer.html`): left rail (five presets with one-line stories, damage segmented control, collapsed custom parameters, reset), a generated headline, four KPI tiles (peak population and year; 2100 population against the standard run; resources left; damage effect on 2100 industry), one main chart (chosen run, dashed grey standard baseline, dotted same-settings damage-off line, peak annotation, direct end labels). Under "Details": three more World3 charts, the FaIR chart and stripes, the AI-race automation and damage charts with their controls, the loop-sign text, the verbatim v0.1 caveat blocks (kept in `site_src/dash_blocks.py`, cut from the old page), and a tests table (A3.1, A3.2, A3.4(b), M6, A2.1, A1.3, A1.4, A2.6, B1, R1, B2) linking to Limitations.

**4. Compare** (`compare.html`): baseline (standard run, damage off) against scenario A and B (a preset and a damage option each), state in the URL hash, generated headline, overlay chart (three lines, metric selector), diverging bars of the 2100 change against the baseline with a thick zero line, delta table with "B minus A" and a note on identical rows (warming, which is the same everywhere because emissions are prescribed), table CSV.

**5. Connections** (`connections.html`): absolutely positioned nodes, seven numbered links (solid built, dotted built-but-withheld, dashed not built) generated from `site_src/links.py`; selecting a link opens the inspector (status, what it does, effect numbers computed from the data for the two built links, tests met, failed, not compared, a Methods link). Tab and arrow keys move between links, Enter or Space selects, the choice is in the hash (`#link=3`); below 900 px the map becomes a list of the same links with the same inspector inline. The old static loop and roadmap diagrams are gone from the site (the map replaces them); `ig.py` keeps their markup, unused apart from the stripes.

**Phones:** single column; Explorer and Compare have a bottom bar Choose story, Result, Reliability (Result first, so the message is visible without a tap).

**Generated text and tests.** Headlines, tiles, deltas, hash state, link effect numbers and CSV live in `core.js` and are tested in Node on the committed data and on synthetic data (`tests/test_site_v02_core.py`, `test_site_connections.py`). `test_site_v02_core.py` also scans every page script for strings derived from the data (peaks, end values, effects, shares) and for years typed next to the word "peak", with a control that proves the scan can fail (extends the D-052 pattern). Structure tests: `test_site_story.py`, `test_site_compare.py`, `test_site_ux.py` (updated), `test_site_r1_sentence.py` (now reads `dashboard.js`). Run: `PYTHONUTF8=1 .venv/Scripts/python.exe -m pytest tests/test_site_*.py -q`.

**Build:** `PYTHONPATH=. python site_src/generate.py --reuse` rebuilds every page and asset from `site/data/explorer.json` (no new run). A full rebuild needs the climate recording as before.

**Checked live** (headless Edge through the DevTools protocol, plus the Chrome extension for the first checks; screenshots in `docs/img/explorer-v02-*-1280.png` and `-390.png`): each page at 1280 and 390 px viewports: no horizontal overflow (the Compare delta table scrolls inside its own labelled region), minimum rendered text 13 px, no console or script errors; story controls; dashboard presets, damage control, hash, Details, data table; the phone step bar; Compare picks, swap, a bad hash, A equal to B equal to baseline; the connections map with the keyboard (arrows, Enter, Space) and the list below 900 px. **Not checked:** a physical phone (the 390 px view is an emulated viewport), browsers other than Edge and the Chrome extension, a screen reader, a colour-blindness simulation, a printed page.

**Choices different from the brief or not stated in it:** the dashboard is its own page (`explorer.html`) and the story is `index.html`; four KPI tiles, not five; the main chart's finding title is the page headline; the phone starts on Result; reading pages follow the system theme, so on a dark system the story opens dark; the AI-race lines overlap (the three settings barely differ in automation timing) so a computed note says how far apart they are; the stripes are an infographic with a text alternative, not a chart with table, PNG and CSV; the Compare table has a CSV but no PNG; "Read this first" is on the story page, the other pages carry the banner and status badges.

## Observed history overlay and warming map (branch `site-history`, D-057 Provisional, 2026-10-22): what changed
Branched from `site-ux`; not merged, not published, the Pages workflow is unchanged. Data and display layer only: no model code, no parameter and no model run changed (the only new computation is a FaIR hindcast with the same published configuration, and a regression of published CMIP6 regional aggregates).
- **Panels and tabs:** "Warming map" (after Climate) and "Model versus reality" (after AI race) are added to the tab bar, each with a status badge, and one line is added to "Read this first". The v0.2 story chapters and dashboard do not exist on `site-ux` (only D-056 is recorded there), so the "one line in story chapter 1" was put in "Read this first"; move it when v0.2 lands.
- **Model versus reality:** World3 population against UN WPP 2024 (1950-2025; hollow dots for the UN's own 2024-2025 projection, not used in the gap); World3 industrial output and World Bank world GDP, both indexed to 1990 = 1 (not the same quantity, stated); FaIR 841-member hindcast band against HadCRUT.5.2.0.0 (1850-2025, re-based to 1850-1900). World3 curves are the damage-off run of the settings chosen in the World3 panel. One sentence per chart is computed by the page (`historyPopGap`, `historyIoGap`, `historyTempGap`), with the readings fixed before the numbers were seen (`docs/history-comparison.md`). A3.4(b) and A2.1 are stated where they apply. A comparison is not a validation, stated on the panel, in Methods and in Limitations.
- **Warming map:** 58 AR6 reference regions (46 land, 12 ocean), coefficient (K per K of global) = multi-model-mean regression slope over 32 CMIP6 models from the IPCC AR6 Atlas regional aggregates (CC BY 4.0), times the recorded FaIR global warming of the chosen year (2025-2100; mean, 5th and 95th percentile beside the map). ColorBrewer OrRd, direct kelvin legend, hover, tap and keyboard focus read-out, table of all regions. Mandatory label and link to Methods are on the panel. Pattern uncertainty is stored (`c_sd`) and not drawn; the page says so. The map follows the climate run only. Method fixed before computing in `docs/warming-map.md`.
- **Files:** `site_src/history_build.py`, `site_src/map_build.py` (data builders; raw downloads stay outside the repo), `site/data/history.json`, `site/data/warming-map.json`, `audit/data/sources-history.json` (source, version, retrieval date, licence quote, URL, sha256), `audit/data/atlas-cmip6-files.json`, `audit/data/warming-map-check.json`, tests `tests/test_site_history.py`, screenshots `docs/img/explorer-{map,history}-{1280,390}.png`.
- **Result of the comparison, as computed:** population 2023 -7.2% (outside the 5% band); output index 2025 -50.7% (outside 25%; different quantities); temperature 2025 FaIR mean 0.07 K below HadCRUT5, outside its 95% interval (HadCRUT5's best estimate is inside the FaIR 5-95% band). Nothing was tuned.
- **Global-mean check of the map:** area-weighted mean coefficient 1.0177 (+1.77%), within the 2% bound but close; with the land-and-sea file for every region 1.0005. Not rescaled.
- **Existing tests changed (structure only):** `tests/test_site_ux.py` expected 3 status badges, 3 "Read this first" lines and the old tab order; now 5, 4 and the order with the two new panels (dated comments in the test). No model test touched.
- **Known issues:** the AR6 regions are coarse (no gridded pattern with a redistributable licence was found); slopes are derived by F3 and are not an IPCC product; WPP licence statement read from the WPP 2024 publication, not from the CSV; GISTEMP licence not found, so only a cross-check number is published.
