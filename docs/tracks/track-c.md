- **CORRECTION of my own first reading (output), made after the other sectors were run, visible and not deleted.** The first S0 run used recorded inputs exported on a 1/16-year grid and linearly interpolated (as the S1 and W3 fixtures are). Its results: `OGR` 2.56% (output TLTL), `IR` (inventory), `ISR` (finance), `UR/UNEM` 11.5% (labour market), `CFWB` 0.34% and others: the S0 run, whose purpose is to test the equations, failed in output TLTL, finance (both) and labour market (both) with errors that vanish when the inputs are the reference's own. Cause (measured): the Euler reference reads its inputs at every 1/64-year step; the 1/16 grid carries them only at every fourth step, so a quantity that is a difference of near-equal numbers (`OGR`, `ISR`, `UNEM`) moves by the interpolation error. **I amended the export** (`audit/t0/earth4all_m4_export.jl`, signals now on the full 1/64-year grid, 12 significant digits; commit `feat: signals on the 1/64-year grid`); this is an amendment of a fixture after seeing a result, labelled here; the tolerance (2%), the criterion sets and the rule were not changed. With 1/64 signals S0 is 0.0000% for every state and headline variable of all 8 sectors, both scenarios (the port reproduces the package's equations to rounding when fed its inputs).
- **Step effect measured (plan A4.1, first part), recorded in `tests/fixtures/e4a_step_effect.json` by `audit/t0/e4a_step_effect.py`** (one run, both scenarios, recorded inputs on the 1/64 grid): S1 (RK4 at 1/64) and S2 (RK4 at 0.25 year, one step) exceed 2% in output (`OGR` 69.1% TLTL in 2090.0, first above 2% 2088.5; 14.4% GL), inventory (`IR` 7.3% TLTL, 11.3% GL, first 1982.5), finance (`ISR` 37.8%, `CBSR` 2.4% TLTL and 18.2% GL, `PU` 2.2%), labour market (`PURA` 29.5% TLTL and 437.6% GL in 1984.0; `UR`, `UNEM` unbounded relative error at points where the reference is near zero), public (`RTFPUA` 2.5% S2 in 2021.0, 3.7% S1), demand (`CFWB` 11.8% from 1983.5, `ETF2022` 2.9% from 2022.5, `CFGB` 2.2%), energy (`FEP`, `UFF` 23.1% S2 in GL, first 2055.0). Inferred, not tested further: the reference is Euler at 1/64 year, so RK4 (more accurate than Euler) departs from the reference exactly where Euler's own error is large (variables near zero or with discontinuous switches: `OGR` and `UNEM` cross zero, `FEP` and `UFF` hit a `max(0, .)` kink in GL, `RTFPUA` starts at 0 and `ETF2022` at the 2022 step). The step effect of the loop step itself is the S2 minus S1 difference, small everywhere (largest `RTFPUA` 1.2 points); the gap to the reference is mostly the reference's own Euler error. `other` is within 0.011% in all runs.

Reason recorded: the reference's own scheme is the only one that reproduces the reference within 2%, because the reference's Euler error exceeds 2% for variables near zero or at kinks; the test A4.1 S3 checks the sector as it runs in the loop (0.25-year loop step, 1/64 internal Euler).
