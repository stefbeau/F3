Publications tagged with OpenAlex’s Artificial intelligence concept rose from 163,844 in 2000 to 1,294,813 in 2024, a factor of 7.9. The count is of tagged works, not a share of all research, so it does not show whether AI’s share of research changed; it also says nothing about why the count rose.

Who publishes

China (2,252,080) and United States (1,847,001) have the two largest counts, equal to 16% and 14% of the 13.7 M works. Countries are taken from the authors’ institutions, so a work with authors in several countries is counted in each of them; the by-country response was cut off at 200 countries. These are counts, not exact shares.

In the F3 model

Nothing on this page feeds the Explorer. The AI-sector model (S3) is unvalidated against GATE after about 2027 (Limitations). Links that would connect AI to other parts of the model are only proposed so far:

Proposed links in the F3 architecture

  • L8 Disaster-economy: extreme weather event damage to GDP and food loss (proposed, not implemented)
  • L9 Climate-migration-labour: displacement to labour supply and skill mix (proposed, not implemented)
  • L_MH Mental health: AI displacement and climate stress to productivity (proposed, not implemented)
  • L_CONF Conflict: resource and climate stress to armed conflict to the economy (proposed, not implemented)
  • L_RISK AI safety: incident rate and capability to catastrophic risk probability (proposed, not implemented)

Five of the ten links proposed in docs/architecture.md (D-060 gap analysis). Each is a proposal: none is built, tested or approved, and none appears on the Connections page, which shows the seven links of the current model.
