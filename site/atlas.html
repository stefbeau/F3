How to read the atlas

Two kinds of numbers. Reported figures come from agencies, studies and news reports, each linked. F3 output comes from the model runs behind the Explorer. They are never placed in the same chart or table.

Reported figures were read on 2026-11-25. Most were read in news reports or summaries, and the original study was not always opened. Projections get revised: the IEA’s 2030 figure for data-centre electricity moved from 945 TWh in April 2025 to about 1,000 TWh in November 2026.

Associations are not causes. Where a study shows only that two things occur together, the page says so. Where a common claim could not be supported, the page says that too.

Most topics are not in the model. F3 output appears on three of the eleven pages. On the other eight the page says plainly that F3 does not model the topic.

Not covered yet: privacy law, effects on children’s development beyond the studies cited, energy and water used in training single models, and regional detail for most topics.
