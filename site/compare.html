How reliable is this?

▲ World3: standard run matches its reference; A3.4(b) failed; changed runs not compared ▲ Climate: A2.1 failed by 0.037 K

  • Each curve is one model run under stated assumptions: no band, no probability, a conditional scenario and not a forecast. The AI-race run is not part of this comparison (it is not connected to World3: S3's economy and World3's are not reconciled).
  • Only the standard parameters with climate damage off were compared with WorldDynamics.jl. Damage-on runs are not compared with WorldDynamics.jl (it has no climate damage), and scenario runs with changed parameters are not compared with anything. High is not the upper end of the literature.
  • No health or yield effect of warming is modelled; warming is the same in the baseline and in both scenarios because emissions are prescribed.
  • The recorded tests and their results are in the table under Details on the Explorer page and on Limitations.
