Inspector

Select a numbered link on the map.

  1. Climate damage multiplies World3's whole industrial output by (1 minus c x T squared) from 2025, with T the recorded FaIR ensemble-mean warming and c one of the three D-007 coefficients. A level-of-output effect, off by default. The change flows through investment, agriculture, resources and population.

  2. The same damage function takes a share of S3's own output, with the recorded FaIR ensemble-mean warming and one of the three D-007 options.

  3. Not built. World3's population and food sectors have no warming term, so warming does not reach them except through industrial output (link 1). The Earth4All channels (life expectancy, land yield, CO2 effect on yield, CO2 lifetime) were left out by decision D-049, to be revisited.

  4. Not built. S3's economy and World3's are two models of the same world and are not reconciled; nothing in the AI-race run changes anything in World3.

  5. Built in the AI-race run: S3 compute sets data-centre electricity demand, and supply limits compute. The outputs are withheld because they leave any plausible range, which is a property of S3's compute path, not a finding about the world.

  6. Not built. Emissions are prescribed, so the climate path is the same for every scenario in the Explorer.

  7. Not built. World3 does not change emissions; the FaIR run replays one recorded path (D-021).

The same links as a feedback loop

A fixed picture of the seven links, numbered as on the map above, with the same line styles. It complements the interactive map: it shows which links close a loop and which do not exist.

  • Solid: built and tested
  • Dotted: built, outputs withheld
  • Dashed: not built
  1. Built, solid: warming lowers World3 industrial output through the D-007 damage function. A level-of-output effect, off by default; set it with the climate-damage control on the Explorer.
  2. Built, solid: warming lowers the AI model's (S3) own output through the same damage function (the AI-race charts under Details on the Explorer).
  3. Not built, dashed: any effect of warming on World3 health or yield. There is no health or yield effect of warming; the Earth4All channels were left out by decision (D-049).
  4. Not built, dashed: the AI model feeding World3. S3's economy and World3's are two models of the same world and are not reconciled.
  5. Built in the AI-race run, outputs withheld, dotted: S3 compute sets data-centre electricity demand and supply limits compute. The values are withheld until S3's compute path is validated.
  6. Not built, dashed: data-centre electricity feeding emissions.
  7. Not built, dashed: World3 feeding emissions. Emissions are prescribed, so the climate path is the same for every scenario in the Explorer.

Two views that are not links between the models

Two views in the Explorer look at the models from outside; neither is a link and neither changes any run.

  • Model versus reality sets World3 and the FaIR hindcast against observed population, world output and temperature. A comparison with history, not a validation.
  • Warming map scales the global FaIR warming (the source of links 1 and 2) to 58 regions with fixed coefficients. Illustrative, not a regional projection; the same for every scenario.

Why most links are not built

  • Emissions are prescribed. The FaIR run replays one recorded emissions path (D-021); neither World3 nor the AI model changes it. The climate is the same in every scenario.
  • S3's economy and World3's are not reconciled. They are two models of the same world; connecting them is future work, not something the Explorer shows.
  • Warming has no health or yield effect. The Earth4All channels were left out by decision (D-049), to be revisited.
  • S3 compute and data-centre electricity outputs are withheld until the S3 compute path is validated (awaiting Epoch AI).
