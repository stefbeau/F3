Inspector

Select a numbered link on the map.

  1. Climate damage multiplies World3's whole industrial output by (1 minus c x T squared) from 2025, with T the recorded FaIR ensemble-mean warming and c one of the three D-007 coefficients. A level-of-output effect, off by default. The change flows through investment, agriculture, resources and population.

  2. The same damage function takes a share of S3's own output, with the recorded FaIR ensemble-mean warming and one of the three D-007 options.

  3. Built, outputs withheld (yield only). From 2025, World3's land yield can be lowered in proportion to warming above the 2025 level, with a low, central and high loss per degree taken from the crop-yield literature (Zhao and others, 2017). It is off by default and the Explorer does not use it: no scenario on this site changes because of it, and no number from it is shown here. Health is not built: a global mean temperature gives no defensible mortality function, so this link is half built. Provisional, awaiting the editor's review (D-069).

  4. Built, outputs withheld. World3's industrial output can be multiplied by S3's uplift (output with AI investment over output without), limited to a yearly growth cap that the person running it must choose; the cap is an assumption, not data. It is off by default and the Explorer does not use it: no scenario on this site changes because of it, and no number from it is shown here. S3's economy and World3's are still not reconciled, and the link is one-way. Provisional, awaiting the editor's review (D-069).

  5. Built: S3 compute sets data-centre electricity demand, and supply limits compute (and, optionally and off by default, the electricity left for other users limits World3 industrial output, D-059). The compute and electricity outputs stay withheld in the Explorer because they leave any plausible range, which is a property of S3's compute path, not a finding about the world.

  6. Built, outputs withheld. The electricity used by data centres above its 2025 level can be turned into extra fossil CO2 with the carbon intensity of the Earth4All reference path, and added to the emissions FaIR reads. It is off by default and the Explorer does not use it: no scenario on this site changes because of it, and no number from it is shown here. In the Explorer, emissions stay prescribed and the climate path is the same for every scenario. One-way. Provisional, awaiting the editor's review (D-069).

  7. Built, outputs withheld. Fossil CO2 from 2026 can be computed from World3's industrial output and a carbon intensity fitted to 1970 to 2024, replacing the prescribed path that FaIR reads. It is off by default and the Explorer does not use it: no scenario on this site changes because of it, and no number from it is shown here. There are two constructions. The first failed its hindcast. The second is a post-hoc proposal, labelled as such: it picks the World3 run closest to observed world output as reference and joins other runs to the 2025 value with one constant. One-way. Provisional, awaiting the editor's review (D-069).

The same links as a feedback loop

A fixed picture of the seven links, numbered as on the map above, with the same line styles. It complements the interactive map: it shows which links close a loop and which do not exist.

  • Solid: built and tested
  • Dashed: not built (none at present)
  • Dotted: built, outputs withheld
  1. Built, solid: warming lowers World3 industrial output through the D-007 damage function. A level-of-output effect, off by default; set it with the climate-damage control on the Explorer.
  2. Built, solid: warming lowers the AI model's (S3) own output through the same damage function (the AI-race charts under Details on the Explorer).
  3. Built, dotted (outputs withheld): warming lowering World3 land yield, off by default and not used by the Explorer. Health is not built. In the Explorer there is no health or yield effect of warming (D-049).
  4. Built, dotted (outputs withheld): the AI model feeding World3 under a growth cap chosen by hand; the cap decides the result. S3's economy and World3's are two models of the same world and are not reconciled.
  5. Built, solid (values withheld): S3 compute sets data-centre electricity demand and supply limits compute. The values are withheld until S3's compute path is validated.
  6. Built, dotted (outputs withheld): data-centre electricity feeding emissions; an upper reading, off by default.
  7. Built, dotted (outputs withheld): World3 feeding emissions, off by default; its first hindcast failed and the second construction is post-hoc. In the Explorer, emissions are prescribed, so the climate path is the same for every scenario.

Two views that are not links between the models

Two views in the Explorer look at the models from outside; neither is a link and neither changes any run.

  • Model versus reality sets World3 and the FaIR hindcast against observed population, world output and temperature. A comparison with history, not a validation.
  • Warming map scales the global FaIR warming (the source of links 1 and 2) to 58 regions with fixed coefficients. Illustrative, not a regional projection; the same for every scenario.

Why four links are built but not shown

  • They are provisional. Links 3, 4, 6 and 7 were built in November 2026 with tests written first. Each is a switch that is off by default, and their results await the editor's review (D-069). Until then no number from them appears on this site.
  • Emissions are prescribed in the Explorer. The FaIR run replays one recorded emissions path (D-021). Links 6 and 7 can change that path in a separate run, but no Explorer scenario uses them, so the climate is the same in every scenario here.
  • S3's economy and World3's are not reconciled. They are two models of the same world. Link 4 passes S3's uplift to World3 only under a growth cap chosen by hand, and the cap decides the result; it is not something the Explorer shows.
  • Warming has no health or yield effect in the Explorer. Link 3 adds a yield effect in a separate run; a health effect is not built. The Earth4All channels were left out by decision (D-049).
  • S3 compute and data-centre electricity outputs (link 5 is built, its values are not shown) are withheld: under F3's own investment rule they leave any plausible range; a build-rate limit is approved (D-066) and not built.
