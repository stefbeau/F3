Step 2 of 4: add the heat

Adding the heat lowers industrial output

The FaIR climate run warms the same way in every scenario here (emissions are prescribed). Switch the climate-damage option on and World3's industrial output is cut by a share that grows with warming squared.

Climate damage to industrial output

Stripes: FaIR mean, not observed; test A2.1 failed (FaIR starts about 0.04 to 0.07 K below observed). The three damage options are level-of-output effects; high is not the upper end of the literature. See Limitations.

▲ Amber: test A2.1 failed by 0.037 K; damage-on runs are not compared with WorldDynamics.jl

Step 3 of 4: the AI race

The AI race automates tasks within years

S3, this project's AI-economy model, rebuilt from the equations of GATE. Only the timing of automation is shown. Levels are flagged red because they are unvalidated.

AI investment setting

■ Red: S3 failed its test against GATE (A1.3); levels are unvalidated, read the shape only

    Read this first

    • Scenarios, not forecasts. Each curve is one model run under stated assumptions. It has no probability and says nothing about what will happen.
    • Only one link between the pieces is built: warming lowers World3 industrial output, through a damage function, switched off by default. The AI model and World3 are two models of the same world and are not connected or reconciled.
    • Check the status badges. The climate run missed its test against observations by a small margin, the AI model's levels are unvalidated, and World3 was compared with its reference only for the standard run. Compute and electricity outputs are withheld.
